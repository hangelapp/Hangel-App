# **App Name**: Hangel

## Core Features:

- Timeline Feed: Display a feed of mini-blog posts from followed NGOs and brands, showing updates relevant to the user's interests and affiliations.
- Marketplace: Enable users to discover brands, cooperatives, and social enterprises with integrated donation options. Brands may wish to incorporate a generative AI tool to respond to user questions.
- QR Code Payments: Integrate a QR code payment system allowing users to donate a percentage of their purchase to NGOs.
- Volunteer Opportunities: Enable NGOs to post volunteer opportunities, and allow users to search/apply based on skills, location, and social interests.
- Events: Allow users to discover and register for events organized by NGOs and brands.
- Emergency Assistance: Provide a one-click emergency assistance feature, connecting users with nearby aid and resources, focusing on blood donation requests initially.
- Impact Score: Show a gamified impact score to the users, computed according to donations, volunteering hours, number of invited active users, and other parameters. Display badges associated with users' engagement.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Coral (#FF7F50) for a soft, inviting feel.
- Background color: Light pink (#F8E8E8) to complement the coral hue with a desaturated tone.
- Accent color: Pale orange (#F08080) to give a contrasting effect, drawing the user's eye.
- Body and headline font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) to maintain readability while providing a modern aesthetic.
- Use simple, Material Design-inspired icons to represent various actions and categories, ensuring consistency.
- Card-based layouts with rounded corners to organize content clearly and align with modern design trends.
- Bottom navigation bar to keep core features always accessible for ergonomic, one-handed use.
