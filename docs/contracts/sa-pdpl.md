## 1. Scope (Article 2)

hangel ("we", "us", "our") provides a mobile and web application (the "Service") in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. The KSA PDPL applies to any processing of personal data of individuals residing in the Kingdom by any means, including processing carried out **outside** the Kingdom by entities offering goods or services to data subjects in the Kingdom. This notice describes how we comply with the PDPL and its Implementing Regulations.

## 2. Personal data we process

We process: (a) identity & account data — full name (per the National ID / Iqama, where you choose to provide it), email, mobile number (typically +966), hashed password, date of birth for age verification; (b) profile & content data — photos, posts, goals; (c) device & technical data — IP address, advertising identifier (where permitted), device model, OS version, crash logs; (d) usage data — feature interactions, session duration; (e) location data — approximate or, with separate consent, precise device location; (f) communications data — support tickets, survey responses; (g) payment data — handled by Apple, Google, Stripe and (where applicable) Mada; we receive only a transaction reference.

We do not knowingly process **sensitive data** (Article 1(8): personal data revealing racial or ethnic origin, religious, intellectual or political belief, security or criminal data, biometric or genetic data, health data, data indicating that an individual has unknown parents) except where strictly necessary and based on **explicit consent** under Article 6(2).

## 3. Lawful bases (Article 6) and the consent default

Unlike the GDPR, the KSA PDPL treats **consent** as the default basis. Other bases include: (i) achievement of a clear interest of the data subject where contacting the data subject is impossible or difficult; (ii) compliance with a legal obligation or judicial order; (iii) performance of an agreement to which the data subject is a party; (iv) processing necessary for a legitimate interest of the controller, *provided this does not prejudice the rights of the data subject* and does not involve sensitive data. Marketing, push notifications, precise location and non-essential cookies require **prior, explicit, opt-in consent**.

## 4. Children

The PDPL does not set a specific age threshold but the Implementing Regulations require parental or guardian consent for minors. We require users in the Kingdom to be **18 years or older** to create an account independently; users under 18 require **verifiable guardian consent**, in line with the Sharia principle that a guardian acts for the minor.

## 5. Cross-border transfers (Article 29) and the EU/Türkiye connection

Transfers of personal data outside the Kingdom are restricted under Article 29 and the Regulation on Personal Data Transfer outside the Kingdom (2023). Transfers are permitted to a country that SDAIA has assessed as providing an **appropriate level of protection** (an evaluation list is being progressively published). Where the destination is not on the list, transfers may proceed only on the basis of: (a) binding common provisions approved by SDAIA; (b) standard contractual clauses issued by SDAIA; (c) certification by an SDAIA-accredited body; or (d) an explicit derogation (e.g. explicit consent, vital interest).

We transfer personal data to: the **United States** (cloud hosting via Google Cloud Platform — `me-central2` Dammam region for resident data, with `us-central1` only as a disaster-recovery target); the **European Union** (analytics tooling under GDPR safeguards); and **Türkiye** (engineering operations of hangel Ltd. Şti., the group parent, under KVKK Law 6698). Until SDAIA publishes adequacy decisions for these jurisdictions, we rely on **SDAIA-aligned standard contractual clauses** and conduct a **Transfer Impact Assessment** for each new transfer in line with the 2023 transfer regulation. We **prioritise residency of resident data** in the `me-central2` Dammam region.

## 6. Your rights (Articles 4 and 9–10 IR)

You have the right to: (i) be informed of the legal or actual justification for collecting your personal data; (ii) access your personal data held by us; (iii) request a copy in a clear and readable format; (iv) request correction, completion or update; (v) request destruction when no longer needed for the purpose collected. We respond to requests within a reasonable period and at no cost (subject to the Implementing Regulations' fee provisions). You may withdraw consent at any time.

## 7. Retention and destruction (Article 18)

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to achieve the purpose for which it was collected, after which we destroy it (Article 18(1)). Exceptions: data may be retained beyond the purpose where required by law (e.g. tax records under the VAT Implementing Regulations — **6 years**; corporate-tax records — **10 years**) or to substantiate a legal claim. Account data is retained for the life of your account plus 24 months for fraud prevention.

## 8. Security and breach notification (Articles 19 and 20)

We implement organisational, administrative and technical measures (encryption, role-based access, audit logs, vendor due diligence, in line with the NDMO Personal Data Protection Standards). Upon becoming aware of a personal-data breach that may cause damage to personal data or the data subject, we notify SDAIA within **72 hours** and notify affected data subjects **without undue delay** where the breach may cause damage to the data subject's data or contradicts their rights or interests.

## 9. Penalties

Penalties under the KSA PDPL are among the highest in the GCC: (i) **imprisonment up to 2 years and/or a fine up to SAR 3 million** for unlawful disclosure or publication of sensitive data with intent to harm or for personal gain (Article 35); (ii) **fines up to SAR 5 million** (doubled on repeat) for other contraventions including unauthorised cross-border transfer (Article 36); (iii) compensation to affected data subjects under the general principles of liability.
