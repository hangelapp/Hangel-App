## 1. Who we are and what this notice covers

hangel ("we", "us", "our") provides a mobile application and related web services (the "Service") that allow users to track personal goals, share content with trusted contacts and access integrated third-party features. This notice explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal data of users in the United Kingdom, in compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) as incorporated by the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 and amended by the Data Protection Act 2018 ("DPA 2018").

This notice applies to all processing carried out by hangel as a **controller** under Part 2, Chapter 2 of the DPA 2018 (general processing). We do not carry out processing for law-enforcement purposes (DPA 2018 Part 3) or intelligence-services purposes (Part 4).

## 2. Personal data we collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

- **Identity & account data** — name, username, email, phone number, password hash, date of birth (for age verification).
- **Profile & content data** — photographs, posts, goals, comments, voice notes you submit to the Service.
- **Device & technical data** — IP address, device identifier (IDFV / Android Advertising ID where permitted), operating-system version, app version, crash logs, language and time-zone.
- **Usage data** — pages viewed, features used, session duration, in-app events.
- **Location data** — approximate (IP-based) or, with your separate consent, precise device location.
- **Communications data** — support tickets, in-app messages, survey responses.
- **Payment data** — handled by Apple App Store / Google Play / Stripe; we receive only a transaction reference, never your full card number.

We do **not** routinely collect special-category data under Article 9 UK GDPR (health, biometrics, religion, sexual orientation, etc.). If you voluntarily upload such data (e.g. a wellness goal that mentions health), processing is based on your explicit consent and you may withdraw it at any time.

## 4. Legitimate-interests assessment (LIA)

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced our interest against your rights and freedoms. Our legitimate interests are: (i) keeping the Service secure and abuse-free; (ii) understanding aggregate usage to improve product quality; (iii) responding to enquiries; and (iv) limited direct marketing of similar services to existing customers (the "soft opt-in" under Regulation 22(3) PECR). A full LIA is available on request to dpo@hangel.org.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies (PECR)

We use strictly-necessary cookies without consent. All other cookies and SDKs (analytics, advertising, social plug-ins) are placed only **after** you give consent via our cookie banner, in line with Regulation 6 PECR and the ICO Cookies Guidance. You can change your preferences at any time via the in-app "Cookie settings" screen.

## 6. Children (under 18)

The minimum age to create a hangel account in the UK is **13**, which is the age of consent for information-society services under section 9 DPA 2018. Where users are between 13 and 17 we apply the **ICO Age Appropriate Design Code ("Children's Code", 15 standards, in force 2 September 2021)**, including: high-privacy default settings, geolocation off by default, no nudge techniques, no profiling for marketing, age-appropriate language and a child-friendly version of this notice at /privacy/kids. We carry out a Children's Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) before launching any feature likely to be accessed by children.

## 7. Recipients and processors

We share personal data with:

- **Cloud infrastructure** — Google Cloud (London region) and Cloudflare (UK PoPs).
- **Communications & analytics** — SendGrid, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics.
- **Payments** — Apple, Google, Stripe Payments UK Ltd.
- **Customer support** — Zendesk (with EU-region data residency).
- **Professional advisers and auditors** — bound by confidentiality.
- **Public authorities** — only where required by UK law (e.g. Schedule 2 Part 1 DPA 2018 exemptions for crime prevention).

All processors are bound by a written contract that meets Article 28 UK GDPR requirements.

## 8. International transfers

Following Brexit, the UK operates its own adequacy regime. Transfers from the UK to the European Economic Area, Gibraltar and other UK-adequate jurisdictions are unrestricted. Transfers to **Türkiye** (where hangel's parent operates engineering) and other non-adequate countries are protected by the **UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)** or the **UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses**, supplemented by a Transfer Risk Assessment (TRA) carried out in accordance with ICO guidance (post-Schrems II). A copy is available from dpo@hangel.org.

## 9. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this notice:

- Account data — for the life of the account plus 30 days after deletion.
- Billing records — 6 years from the end of the relevant tax year (s. 386 Companies Act 2006).
- Marketing-consent records — until consent is withdrawn plus 2 years (audit trail).
- Server logs — 90 days.

## 10. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to: (a) be informed; (b) access (Subject Access Request — SAR); (c) rectification; (d) erasure ("right to be forgotten"); (e) restrict processing; (f) data portability; (g) object, including to direct marketing; and (h) rights related to automated decision-making and profiling under Article 22.

To make a request, email **dpo@hangel.org** or write to our registered office. We will respond within **one calendar month** (extendable by two months for complex requests, with reasons). SARs are free; we may charge a reasonable fee for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests, as permitted by Article 12(5) UK GDPR.

## 11. Right to lodge a complaint

If you are not satisfied with our response you can complain to the **ICO** at https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ or by calling the ICO helpline on 0303 123 1113. You may also seek a judicial remedy.

## 12. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (Article 32 UK GDPR), including encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256), least-privilege access, MFA for staff, an information-security management system aligned with ISO/IEC 27001, and a documented incident-response plan. Personal-data breaches likely to result in a risk to your rights are notified to the ICO within 72 hours under Article 33 UK GDPR.

## 13. Automated decision-making

We do not subject users to decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing.

## 14. Changes to this notice

Material changes will be notified in-app at least 30 days before they take effect.
