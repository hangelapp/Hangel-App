## 1. Scope (適用範囲)

hangel ("we", "us", "our") provides a mobile and web application (the "Service") in Japan. We act as a *personal information handling business operator* under article 16 of the APPI. This notice describes how we handle personal information (個人情報), personal data (個人データ), retained personal data (保有個人データ) and personal-related information (個人関連情報) of users in Japan.

## 2. Categories of personal information collected (取得する個人情報)

We collect: (a) identity & account data — name, email, phone, hashed password, date of birth for age verification; (b) profile & content data — photos, posts, goals; (c) device & technical data — IP address, advertising identifier (where permitted), device model, OS version, crash logs; (d) usage data — feature interactions, session duration; (e) location data — approximate or, with separate consent, precise device location; (f) communications data — support tickets, survey responses; (g) payment data — handled by Apple, Google and Stripe; we receive only a transaction reference.

We do not knowingly collect *special-care-required personal information* (要配慮個人情報, article 2(3) APPI — race, creed, social status, medical history, criminal record, having been the victim of a crime) unless you voluntarily upload such information, in which case processing is based on **prior, opt-in consent** under article 20(2).

## 3. Purposes of use (利用目的)

Under article 17 we specify the following purposes of use as precisely as possible: (i) account registration and authentication; (ii) provision and maintenance of the Service; (iii) customer support; (iv) safety, security and fraud prevention; (v) product analytics and quality improvement; (vi) compliance with legal obligations; (vii) — only with separate opt-in consent — marketing communications and personalised recommendations. We will not use personal information beyond these purposes without your prior consent under article 18.

## 4. Cross-border transfers (外国にある第三者への提供) and the EU/Türkiye connection

We transfer personal data to third parties located outside Japan, including the **United States** (cloud hosting via Google Cloud Platform — `asia-northeast1` and `us-central1` regions), the **European Union** (analytics tooling under EU GDPR safeguards; the EU has been recognised as providing an equivalent level of protection under the 2019 mutual adequacy decision with Japan) and **Türkiye** (engineering operations of hangel Ltd. Şti., the group parent). For transfers to countries without a PIPC-recognised equivalent regime, we obtain your **prior opt-in consent** under article 28 and provide you with information on the data-protection regime of the destination country (per the 2022 amendment). Türkiye is not currently on the PIPC equivalence list, so transfers there are made under contractual clauses requiring measures equivalent to the APPI.

## 5. Outsourcing (委託) and joint use (共同利用)

Where we outsource processing (article 27(5)(i)) — e.g. cloud hosting, SMS gateways — we conduct necessary and appropriate supervision of the trustee. We do not currently engage in joint use (共同利用) under article 27(5)(iii); if we begin to do so we will publish the items, scope, purposes and the name of the person responsible for management before commencement.

## 6. Children (未成年者)

Although there is no statutory age threshold in the APPI itself, the PIPC's Q&A 1-62 recommends obtaining consent from a person with parental authority for users **under 15**. Our Service requires users to be **15 years or older** in Japan to create an account on their own. Users aged 13–14 may register only with verifiable parental consent.

## 7. Rights of the data subject (本人の権利)

Under articles 32 to 39 you may request: (i) disclosure of retained personal data (開示請求), including in electromagnetic form; (ii) correction, addition or deletion (訂正等); (iii) cessation of use or erasure (利用停止・消去); (iv) cessation of third-party provision (第三者提供の停止); (v) disclosure of records of third-party provision. We respond within a reasonable period (we target 14 days). A handling fee may apply only where we incur reasonable costs.

## 8. Security control measures (安全管理措置)

We implement organisational, human, physical and technical security measures consistent with the PIPC Guidelines (Tsūsoku-hen, section 8): documented internal rules, role-based access, training, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logs and vendor due diligence. We publish a summary of our security control measures as required by article 32 paragraph 1 item 4.

## 9. Mandatory breach notification (漏えい等報告)

Since 1 April 2022, breaches involving special-care-required information, risk of property damage, malicious intent, or affecting more than 1,000 individuals must be reported to the PIPC and notified to affected individuals. We report a preliminary notice **within 3–5 days** (sokuhō / 速報) and a definitive report within **30 days (60 days for malicious breaches)**. Non-compliance may attract criminal penalties of up to **1 year imprisonment or JPY 1,000,000**, and corporate fines under article 184 of up to **JPY 100,000,000 (one hundred million yen)**.
