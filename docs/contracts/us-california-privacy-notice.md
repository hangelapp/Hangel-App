## 1. Scope and purpose

This Privacy Notice supplements our global Privacy Policy and applies solely to **California residents** ("consumers", "you") whose Personal Information ("PI") we process as a "business" under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (collectively, "**CCPA**", Cal. Civ. Code §§ 1798.100 et seq.), and its implementing regulations (Cal. Code Regs. tit. 11, §§ 7000–7304).

It serves as our **Notice at Collection** under § 1798.100(b) and is presented at or before the point of collection by linking from the hangel mobile-app sign-up screen and the website footer.

## 3. "Sale" and "Sharing"

We do **not sell** Personal Information for monetary consideration. We may "share" (as defined in § 1798.140(ah), i.e. disclose for **cross-context behavioral advertising**) hashed identifiers and limited analytics events with advertising partners (e.g. Google Ads, Meta) — but **only if you have first granted opt-in consent** through our consent banner. You may withdraw consent at any time via the **"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information"** link in the footer of every page or via the in-app "Privacy choices" screen. We also honor the **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** browser signal as a valid opt-out request under § 7025.

## 4. Sensitive Personal Information

We **limit the use** of Sensitive PI to the purposes permitted by § 7027(m) (e.g. providing the Service you requested, security, short-term transient use, quality control). We do **not** use it to infer characteristics about you. You may exercise your right to limit the use of Sensitive PI via the **"Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information"** link in the footer.

## 5. Purposes of collection

We use Personal Information to:

1. Provide, maintain and secure the Service.
2. Process subscriptions, payments and refunds.
3. Communicate with you about your account.
4. Personalise content and recommendations (no automated decisions with legal/similarly-significant effects).
5. Detect fraud, abuse and security incidents.
6. Comply with legal obligations (tax, accounting, court orders).
7. With consent: send marketing and serve targeted ads.

## 6. Retention

We retain each category for as long as necessary to fulfil the disclosed purpose, then delete or de-identify it. Indicative periods:

- Account data — life of account + 30 days
- Payment records — 7 years (IRS / California Franchise Tax Board)
- Marketing-consent logs — until withdrawal + 2 years
- Server logs — 90 days
- Backups — overwritten on a 35-day rolling cycle

## 7. Your CCPA/CPRA rights

California residents have the right to:

1. **Know** what PI we collect, use, disclose and share (right of access; up to 24 months of look-back, extended from the original 12-month rule by CPRA § 1798.130(a)(2)(B)).
2. **Delete** PI we collected from you (subject to statutory exceptions in § 1798.105(d)).
3. **Correct** inaccurate PI (§ 1798.106).
4. **Opt-out of sale or sharing** (§§ 1798.120, 1798.135).
5. **Limit use of Sensitive PI** (§ 1798.121).
6. **Data portability** in a structured, machine-readable format.
7. **Non-retaliation** for exercising any of the above (§ 1798.125) — we do not deny, charge a different price or provide a different quality of service.
8. **Opt-out of automated decision-making and profiling** once the CPPA's ADMT regulations enter into force.

### How to exercise rights

- Web form: https://hangel.org/privacy/request
- Toll-free: 1-844-HANGEL-1
- Email: privacy@hangel.org
- Authorised agent: submit a power-of-attorney plus the consumer's signed permission; we will verify the consumer's identity directly.

We verify requests using a **two-data-point match** for standard requests and a **three-data-point match plus signed declaration under penalty of perjury** for deletion or Sensitive-PI requests (per § 7062). We respond within **45 calendar days**, extendable once by a further 45 days with written notice.

## 8. Notice of financial incentive

We currently do **not** offer financial incentives or price/service differences in exchange for the retention or sale of PI under § 1798.125(b). If we do in future, we will publish a separate Notice of Financial Incentive.

## 9. B2B and HR data

CPRA removed the original CCPA exemptions for B2B and employee data as of **1 January 2023**. Accordingly, this notice applies in full to: (a) job applicants, employees and contractors of hangel based in California (see our separate **California HR Privacy Notice** at /privacy/hr-ca); and (b) business contacts (e.g. customer-employees acting for a corporate account).

## 10. Children under 16

We do **not knowingly** sell or share the PI of consumers under 16. For consumers between 13 and 15, "sale" or "sharing" requires **opt-in** consent from the consumer; for consumers under 13, **verifiable parental consent** is required under § 1798.120(c) and COPPA (see our separate COPPA Notice). Our sign-up flow gates registration at age 13+ in the US.

## 11. Annual metrics (§ 999.317(g) / CPRA § 7102)

Because we receive PI of 10 million or more California residents in a calendar year, we will publish, on or before 1 July each year, the following statistics for the prior calendar year at https://hangel.org/privacy/ccpa-metrics:

- Number of requests to know received, complied with in whole or in part, and denied.
- Number of requests to delete received, complied with in whole or in part, and denied.
- Number of requests to correct received, complied with in whole or in part, and denied.
- Number of requests to opt-out of sale/sharing received, complied with, and denied.
- Number of requests to limit use of Sensitive PI received, complied with, and denied.
- Median and mean number of days within which we substantively responded.

If we receive PI of fewer than 10 million Californians, this section becomes informational.

## 12. Service-provider and contractor terms

All vendors that receive PI on our behalf sign a written contract that (a) limits their use of PI to the specified business purposes, (b) prohibits sale or sharing, (c) prohibits combination with PI from other sources except as permitted by § 7050(d), and (d) grants us audit rights — meeting the requirements of § 1798.140(ag), (j).
