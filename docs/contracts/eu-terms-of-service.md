## 2. About hangel

hangel operates a digital service that:

- allows hospitals, NGOs and individuals to publish requests for blood donation;
- allows verified individuals to register as donors and respond to requests;
- hosts a knowledge library and AI assistant for donation-related questions;
- provides tooling for hospitals and NGOs to organise donation campaigns.

hangel is a hosting service and online platform within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (the Digital Services Act, "DSA"). hangel does **not** provide medical advice and is **not** a substitute for a clinical decision; donation eligibility is determined by the medical staff at the donation site under applicable national rules.

## 6. Notice-and-Action (Article 16 DSA)

Any person can notify hangel of content they consider illegal:

- in-app: any content card → "Report" → "Illegal content";
- web: https://hangel.org/report;
- email: report@hangel.org.

Please include (a) the reason the content is alleged to be illegal, (b) the precise location (URL or content ID), (c) your contact details, and (d) a good-faith statement that your notice is accurate. You will receive an acknowledgement and a decision with reasons.

If you are the recipient who provided the reported content, you will receive a **statement of reasons** under Article 17 DSA and may use the internal complaint-handling system (§12), the out-of-court dispute settlement bodies (§13), or judicial remedies.

## 7. Content Moderation and Recommender System Transparency

- **Moderation:** hangel removes or restricts content that violates §5 or applicable law. Decisions consider the gravity of the violation, the user's history, and contextual factors.
- **Recommender system:** Where hangel ranks donation requests for you, the **main parameters** are (i) blood-type compatibility, (ii) geographic distance, (iii) recency of last donation, (iv) urgency stated by the requestor, and (v) availability you have indicated. You can change these parameters in Settings → Discovery.
- **No profiling-based advertising:** hangel does not currently show third-party advertising. If it ever does, it will never target ads to you based on special categories of personal data (Article 9 GDPR) or to users it knows or should reasonably know to be minors.
