# Spain — LOPDGDD Supplement (Ley Orgánica de Protección de Datos Personales y Garantía de los Derechos Digitales)

**⚠️ BORRADOR — No constituye asesoramiento legal.** Revisión obligatoria por abogado colegiado en España especializado en protección de datos antes de uso en producción. Última actualización: 2026-06-03.

---

## 1. Legal Framework and Scope (Marco normativo)

The **Ley Orgánica 3/2018, de 5 de diciembre, de Protección de Datos Personales y garantía de los derechos digitales (LOPDGDD)** complements the GDPR in Spain and adds a catalogue of "digital rights" (Título X). Constitutional rank as Ley Orgánica gives certain provisions enhanced protection.

Scope mirrors GDPR Art. 3 plus Art. 2 LOPDGDD; the law also regulates rights of deceased persons (Art. 3 LOPDGDD) — heirs may exercise access, erasure, and rectification subject to the deceased's prior wishes.

## 2. AEPD — Agencia Española de Protección de Datos

- Independent supervisory authority for the entire Spanish territory (state level).
- Regional authorities: **APDCAT (Catalunya)**, **AVPD/EAB (País Vasco)**, **CTBG–APDCM** historically (Madrid abolished 2012). Competence over public-sector files of regional administrations.
- Website: **aepd.es**. Online complaints (canal prioritario), breach notifications, DPO registration.
- Active enforcement: large fines on banks (BBVA €5M, CaixaBank €6M), telcos (Vodafone España €8.15M), Google (€10M, 2022). Tendency to publish detailed sanction resolutions.

## 3. Children's Digital Consent — Age 14

Spain opted down to **14 years** (Art. 7 LOPDGDD). Under 14: parental/guardian consent required. Verification mechanisms must be proportionate; declarative age gates are insufficient for high-risk processing.

## 4. DPO (Delegado de Protección de Datos)

Art. 34 LOPDGDD lists **mandatory DPO** scenarios beyond GDPR Art. 37, including:
- Professional associations (colegios profesionales).
- Educational centres (public and private offering EU-regulated qualifications).
- Public and private universities.
- Electricity and natural gas operators.
- Credit institutions and financial entities.
- Insurance/reinsurance entities.
- Investment services firms.
- Electricity, water, gas distributors.
- Health centres legally obliged to maintain clinical records (excluding solo practitioners).
- Private security companies.
- Gambling operators via electronic means.
- Credit information system operators.
- Advertising and commercial prospecting entities engaged in profiling.

**DPO must be notified to AEPD within 10 days of appointment, modification, or dismissal** (Art. 37 LOPDGDD).

## 5. Workers' Data and Digital Rights at Work (Arts. 87–91 LOPDGDD)

LOPDGDD introduces a worker-rights chapter — among the strongest in the EU:
- **Art. 87 — Right to privacy and use of digital devices at work:** employees have privacy expectations over corporate devices unless the employer has issued a clear use policy.
- **Art. 88 — Right to digital disconnection (derecho a la desconexión digital):** workers may disconnect outside working hours; companies must adopt an internal policy after consulting workers' representatives.
- **Art. 89 — Video surveillance and sound recording:** restricted; must be informed in advance; audio capture only for serious risks; cannot record changing rooms or rest areas.
- **Art. 90 — Geolocation of workers:** only for legitimate purposes related to work; prior information required.
- **Art. 91 — Collective bargaining rights:** these rights may be developed via colectivos (CBAs).

## 6. LSSI-CE — Information Society and E-Commerce

The **Ley 34/2002 de Servicios de la Sociedad de la Información y de Comercio Electrónico (LSSI-CE)** governs cookies and electronic communications:
- **Art. 22.2 LSSI:** cookie consent — explicit, informed, freely given (transposes ePrivacy Art. 5(3)).
- **AEPD Guía sobre el uso de las cookies (versión 2023):** Accept/Reject parity required; no scroll-as-consent; cookie wall only with paid alternative under strict conditions.
- **Art. 21 LSSI:** soft-spam exception — prior commercial relationship for similar products, with opt-out at collection.

## 7. ENS — Esquema Nacional de Seguridad

**Real Decreto 311/2022** updates the ENS. Mandatory for the **public sector** and recommended for private contractors providing services to the public sector. Three categories (Basic, Medium, High) based on impact on confidentiality/integrity/availability/authenticity/traceability. Relevant for hangel if contracting with Spanish public administrations.

## 8. Digital Rights (Título X LOPDGDD — non-exhaustive)

- Art. 79: Right to internet neutrality.
- Art. 80: Right to universal internet access.
- Art. 81: Right to digital security.
- Art. 82: Right to digital education.
- Art. 83: Protection of minors on the internet (parental control).
- Art. 84: Right to rectification on the internet.
- Art. 85: Right to update information in digital media.
- Art. 86: Right to privacy and use of digital devices.
- Art. 93: Right to portability in social networks and equivalent services.
- Art. 94: Right to digital testament.
- Art. 95: Right of access protection.
- Art. 96: Right to digital will (testamento digital).

## 9. Sanctions

Per Arts. 70–78 LOPDGDD:
- Very serious infractions: up to €20M / 4% turnover (mirrors GDPR).
- Serious: up to €10M / 2% turnover.
- Minor: €40,000 maximum with warnings.
- Statute of limitations: 3 years (very serious), 2 (serious), 1 (minor).
