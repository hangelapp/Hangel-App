## 1. Scope

hangel ("we", "us", "our") provides a mobile and web application (the "Service") in the United Arab Emirates. The PDPL applies to controllers and processors residing or located within the State that process personal data of any data subject inside or outside the State, and to controllers/processors **outside** the State that process personal data of data subjects **inside** the State (Article 2(1)). If you are located in the DIFC or ADGM free zones, the respective free-zone data-protection law applies in place of the PDPL.

## 2. Personal data we process

We process: (a) identity & account data — name, email, mobile number, hashed password, date of birth for age verification; (b) profile & content data — photos, posts, goals; (c) device & technical data — IP address, advertising identifier (where permitted), device model, OS version, crash logs; (d) usage data — feature interactions, session duration; (e) location data — approximate or, with separate consent, precise device location; (f) communications data — support tickets, survey responses; (g) payment data — handled by Apple, Google and Stripe; we receive only a transaction reference.

We do not knowingly process **sensitive personal data** (Article 1: ethnicity, political opinions, religious beliefs, criminal record, biometric data, genetic data, health data) unless you voluntarily upload such data, in which case processing is based on **explicit consent** under Article 5(1) and Article 6(2).

## 3. Lawful bases (Article 4 PDPL)

We process personal data on the following bases: (i) consent of the data subject; (ii) performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party; (iii) compliance with a legal obligation; (iv) protection of public interest; (v) legitimate interest of the controller that does not prejudice the data subject's rights. Marketing communications, push notifications, precise location and non-essential cookies are processed only on **freely-given, specific, informed, unambiguous, written or electronic consent** (Article 6), which may be withdrawn at any time.

## 4. Children

The Service requires users to be **18 years or older in the UAE** to create an account on their own, in line with the conservative reading of the PDPL Executive Regulations (consent of the legal guardian is required for any minor under 18). Where age verification indicates a user is under 18, account creation is gated by a parental consent flow with documentary verification.

## 5. Cross-border transfers (Articles 22–23) and the EU/Türkiye connection

We transfer personal data to recipients outside the UAE, including the **United States** (cloud hosting via Google Cloud Platform — `me-central1` Dubai and `us-central1` regions), the **European Union** (analytics tooling under GDPR safeguards) and **Türkiye** (engineering operations of hangel Ltd. Şti., the group parent, under KVKK Law 6698). Under Article 22, transfers may take place to a State that ensures an **adequate level of protection** (an adequacy list will be published by the UAE Data Office). For transfers to non-adequate States we rely on Article 23: (a) explicit consent; (b) standard contractual clauses approved by the UAE Data Office; (c) intra-group binding rules; or (d) contractual safeguards approved on a case-by-case basis. We have entered into intra-group data-transfer agreements with hangel Ltd. Şti. in Türkiye and our processors in the US that incorporate clauses substantially equivalent to the EU SCCs 2021.

## 6. Your rights (Articles 13–18)

You have the right to: (i) request information about how we process your personal data; (ii) request access to and a copy of your data; (iii) request rectification of inaccurate data; (iv) request erasure; (v) request restriction of processing; (vi) object to processing; (vii) request data portability in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format; (viii) withdraw consent at any time; (ix) object to decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal or significant effects. We respond to requests within a reasonable period; if we cannot comply, we will inform you of the reasons and your right to escalate to the UAE Data Office.

## 7. Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected (Article 9). Account data is retained for the life of your account plus 24 months for fraud prevention and legal claims. Server access logs are retained for 12 months. We retain financial records as required by UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 on Corporate Tax and the VAT Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 (typically **5 years** for VAT records, **7 years** under corporate-tax rules).

## 8. Security and breach notification (Articles 20–21)

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures (encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, audit logs, vendor due diligence). In the event of a personal-data breach likely to prejudice the privacy, confidentiality and security of data subjects, we notify the UAE Data Office and affected individuals **without undue delay** upon becoming aware, providing details of the breach, categories of data affected, mitigating measures and contact information for the DPO.

## 9. Penalties

The PDPL itself does not yet specify the level of administrative fines (these will be set out in the Executive Regulations). In the DIFC, the Commissioner may impose fines of up to **USD 100,000 per contravention** under Article 62 DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020. In the ADGM, the OCDP may impose administrative fines of up to **USD 28 million** under Schedule 3 of the ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021. Mainland UAE penalties under the forthcoming Executive Regulations are expected to be substantial; criminal sanctions may also apply under Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 on Combating Rumours and Cybercrimes.
