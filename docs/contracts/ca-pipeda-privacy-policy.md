## 1. Scope and applicable law

hangel ("we", "us", "our") provides a mobile and web application (the "Service") in Canada. We handle personal information in the course of commercial activities and are therefore subject to PIPEDA across Canada, except in provinces with substantially similar private-sector legislation: British Columbia (PIPA BC), Alberta (PIPA AB) and Quebec (Law 25). In Quebec, the stricter rules in this notice (including the 72-hour breach reporting timeline, the right to data portability and the obligation to conduct a Privacy Impact Assessment for cross-border transfers) take precedence.

## 2. The ten PIPEDA fair-information principles

We design the Service around the ten principles in Schedule 1 of PIPEDA: (1) Accountability, (2) Identifying Purposes, (3) Consent, (4) Limiting Collection, (5) Limiting Use, Disclosure and Retention, (6) Accuracy, (7) Safeguards, (8) Openness, (9) Individual Access and (10) Challenging Compliance. Our designated Privacy Officer is accountable for compliance and can be reached at privacy@hangel.org.

## 3. Personal information we collect

We collect: (a) **identity & account data** — name, email, phone number, hashed password, date of birth for age verification; (b) **profile & content data** — photographs, posts, goals you submit; (c) **device & technical data** — IP address, advertising identifier (where permitted), device model, OS version, app version, crash logs; (d) **usage data** — feature interactions, session duration; (e) **location data** — approximate (IP-based) and, with separate consent, precise device location; (f) **communications data** — support tickets and survey responses; (g) **payment data** — handled by Apple App Store, Google Play and Stripe; we receive only a transaction reference.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information (health, biometric, religious belief, sexual orientation) unless you voluntarily upload it, in which case processing is based on **express consent** that you may withdraw at any time. Under Quebec Law 25, biometric data requires prior notification to the CAI before collection.

## 4. Purposes and consent

We identify the purposes for collection at or before the time of collection (Principle 2). For account creation, security and service delivery, consent is implied at sign-up. For optional marketing, push notifications, precise location and non-essential analytics, we obtain **express, granular, opt-in consent**, in line with CASL (the Canadian Anti-Spam Law) for electronic messages and with Law 25 article 8.1 for cookies and tracking technologies.

Children under **14 in Quebec** and under **13 elsewhere in Canada** cannot create an account without parental consent. In Quebec, consent must be given by the holder of parental authority for any minor under 14, in line with Law 25 article 4.1.

## 5. Cross-border transfers and the EU/Türkiye connection

We transfer personal information to processors located in the United States (cloud hosting via Google Cloud Platform — `northamerica-northeast1` and `us-central1`), in the European Union (analytics tooling) and in **Türkiye** (engineering operations of hangel Ltd. Şti., the group parent). For Quebec residents, before each new cross-border transfer we conduct a **Privacy Impact Assessment (PIA)** under Law 25 article 17 and we put in place contractual safeguards equivalent to those required by PIPEDA Principle 4.1.3 (e.g. standard contractual clauses, encryption in transit and at rest, vendor audits). A summary of our PIAs is available on request to privacy@hangel.org.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We retain account data for the lifetime of your account plus 24 months after closure for fraud prevention and legal claims (consistent with the limitation period under most provincial civil codes). Server access logs are retained for 12 months. Quebec residents may exercise the new **right to data portability** under Law 25 article 27, and we will deliver an exportable copy within 30 days.

## 7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, withdrawal of consent, deletion (the new **right to de-indexation / cessation of dissemination** in Quebec) and portability. We respond within 30 days. Complaints may be filed with the OPC (federal) or the CAI (Quebec). Failure to comply with Law 25 may attract administrative monetary penalties of up to **CAD 10 million or 2% of worldwide turnover**, whichever is higher. PIPEDA breaches may result in fines up to **CAD 100,000 per violation**.

## 8. Security and breach notification

We maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the information (encryption, role-based access, audit logs, vendor due diligence). In the event of a breach posing a *real risk of significant harm* we notify the OPC and, where applicable, the CAI **without unreasonable delay** (within 72 hours under Law 25 article 3.5) and notify affected individuals as soon as feasible. We keep a breach register for 24 months as required by PIPEDA.
