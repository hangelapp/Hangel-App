## 1. Why we are giving you this notice

The federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act ("**COPPA**", 15 U.S.C. §§ 6501–6506) and the FTC's implementing **COPPA Rule** (16 C.F.R. Part 312) require operators of online services that are either (a) **directed to children under 13** or (b) **general-audience services with actual knowledge** that they are collecting personal information from a child under 13 to provide a direct notice to parents and obtain **verifiable parental consent (VPC)** before collecting, using or disclosing such information.

The hangel mobile app is a **general-audience** service. Our registration flow gates account creation at **age 13 or older** in the United States (16+ in the EU/Türkiye, with parental consent below 16). However, if we ever become aware that we have collected personal information from a US child under 13 without VPC, this notice describes how we will respond.

## 2. Definitions (16 C.F.R. § 312.2)

- **Child** — an individual under the age of 13.
- **Collection** — request, prompt, encourage submission, or enable public posting, of personal information from a child.
- **Personal information** — first/last name, address, email, phone, SSN, persistent identifier, photo, video, audio containing the child's image or voice, geolocation precise enough to identify a street and city, or any other identifier the FTC designates.
- **Operator** — any person who operates a website or online service and collects personal information from children, or on whose behalf such information is collected.
- **Disclosure** — release of personal information to any third party other than an operator or its service providers.

## 3. Information we would collect from a child (if any)

Because hangel is **not directed to children**, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Should we, despite our age-gate, receive such information, the maximum categories that could be involved are:

- First name or chosen username, password.
- Parent's email address (for VPC).
- Device identifier (IDFV / Android Ad ID — only with consent).
- App-usage analytics (page views, taps).

We do **not** collect: real-time location, contact lists, photos/videos containing the child, audio recordings, or behavioral-advertising identifiers from anyone we know or have reason to know is under 13.

## 4. How we use the information

If we identify a registrant as being under 13, we **suspend the account immediately** and use the limited information solely to:

1. Contact the parent or guardian to obtain VPC or to delete the account.
2. Protect the security and integrity of the Service.
3. Comply with legal obligations.

We do not condition a child's participation in any activity on disclosing more personal information than is reasonably necessary (§ 312.7).

## 5. Disclosure

We do **not** disclose children's personal information to third parties for the third party's own use. The only recipients are:

- Service providers that are contractually bound to use the data solely to perform services for hangel and to maintain confidentiality and security (§ 312.8).
- Law enforcement, where required by lawful process or to protect the safety of a child.

## 6. Verifiable Parental Consent (VPC)

Before we knowingly collect, use or disclose personal information from a child, we obtain VPC from a parent or legal guardian using one of the methods set out in § 312.5(b)(2):

- Signed consent form returned by postal mail, fax or electronic scan.
- Credit-card, debit-card or other online-payment transaction that provides notification of each separate transaction to the primary account holder (a US $0.50 charge is made and immediately refunded).
- Toll-free telephone call to trained personnel at 1-844-HANGEL-1.
- Video-conference with trained personnel.
- Government-issued photo-ID verification, matched against a database and then deleted within a reasonable time.
- Knowledge-based authentication (multiple-choice questions of sufficient difficulty).
- The "**email-plus**" mechanism is **only** used for internal-use cases under § 312.5(b)(2)(ii); for any disclosure to third parties we use one of the stronger methods above.

We keep a record of the consent obtained for as long as the child remains a user, plus a reasonable period thereafter, and make it available to the FTC on request.

## 7. Parental rights

A parent or legal guardian may at any time:

1. Review the personal information collected from their child.
2. Direct us to delete the child's personal information.
3. Refuse to permit further collection or use, while still allowing participation in activities where personal information is not strictly required (§ 312.6).

To exercise these rights, write to **kids-privacy@hangel.org** with a description of the account and proof of guardianship (e.g. copy of birth certificate, court order, or notarised statement). We respond within 10 business days and complete deletion within 30 days.

## 8. Accidental collection — deletion procedure

If we learn that a child under 13 has registered in violation of our age-gate, we will:

1. Immediately suspend the account and disable all data collection.
2. Within 10 business days, attempt to notify a parent at the email provided.
3. Within 30 days, delete all personal information collected from the child, except minimal records necessary to demonstrate compliance with COPPA (kept under access controls and deleted on a 12-month rolling cycle).
4. Log the incident in our COPPA register.

## 9. Data-retention and deletion (§ 312.10)

We retain children's personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, after which it is deleted using reasonable measures to protect against unauthorised access or use during the deletion process.

## 10. Security (§ 312.8)

We use reasonable procedures to protect the confidentiality, security and integrity of children's personal information, including: encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256); role-based access controls; MFA for staff; annual penetration testing; and vendor due-diligence reviews. Vendors handling children's data sign data-processing addenda requiring the same safeguards.

## 11. Safe-Harbor program

hangel intends to participate in an FTC-approved COPPA **Safe Harbor program** (see § 312.11). We are in the application phase for the **kidSAFE Seal Program** (PRIVO and ESRB Privacy Certified are alternative recognised programs). Once approved, certified disputes may be submitted through the Safe-Harbor program in addition to the FTC.

## 12. Enforcement

COPPA is enforced by the **Federal Trade Commission** (https://www.ftc.gov/coppa) and by **State Attorneys General** under 15 U.S.C. § 6504. Civil penalties currently reach **US $51,744 per violation** (adjusted annually for inflation; see 16 C.F.R. § 1.98). Parents may also report concerns to the FTC at https://reportfraud.ftc.gov.

## 13. Changes to this notice

We will obtain new VPC if we materially change the practices to which a parent previously consented. Otherwise, we will post the updated notice and notify parents by email at least 30 days before changes take effect.
