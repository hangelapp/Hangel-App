## 1. Scope

hangel ("we", "us", "our") provides a mobile and web application (the "Service") in Singapore and is an "organisation" under section 2 of the PDPA. This notice describes how we collect, use, disclose and protect your personal data in line with the **nine data-protection obligations** of the PDPA: Consent, Notification, Purpose Limitation, Accuracy, Protection, Retention Limitation, Transfer Limitation, Access & Correction, and Accountability — together with the **Data Breach Notification** and **Data Portability** obligations introduced by the 2020 amendments.

## 2. Personal data we collect

We collect: (a) identity & account data — name, email, mobile number, hashed password, date of birth for age verification; (b) profile & content data — photos, posts, goals; (c) device & technical data — IP address, advertising identifier (where permitted), device model, OS version, crash logs; (d) usage data — feature interactions, session duration; (e) location data — approximate or, with separate consent, precise device location; (f) communications data — support tickets, survey responses; (g) payment data — handled by Apple, Google and Stripe; we receive only a transaction reference.

We do not knowingly collect **sensitive personal data** (NRIC numbers, FIN, passport numbers, financial information, medical records) except where strictly necessary and permitted under the PDPC's *Advisory Guidelines on the PDPA for NRIC and Other National Identification Numbers* (2018, updated 2024). We do not collect NRIC numbers for account registration.

## 3. Consent and notification

We rely primarily on **express or deemed consent** (sections 13–15 PDPA) to collect, use and disclose personal data. Where you submit personal data voluntarily for an obvious purpose (e.g. typing your email into a sign-up field), consent is deemed under section 15. For marketing, push notifications and non-essential cookies we obtain **opt-in express consent**. For limited fraud-prevention, security and product-improvement purposes we may rely on the **Legitimate Interests** or **Business Improvement** exceptions in the First Schedule (paragraphs 1 and 5) introduced by the 2020 amendments, after conducting a documented assessment.

## 4. Children

The Service requires users to be **13 years or older** in Singapore (consistent with the PDPC's Advisory Guidelines on the PDPA for Children's Personal Data of August 2023). For users aged 13 to under 18, we treat consent as effective if the child has sufficient understanding of the consequences. Parental consent is required for users under 13.

## 5. Transfer outside Singapore (section 26 PDPA) and the EU/Türkiye connection

We transfer personal data to recipients outside Singapore, namely the **United States** (cloud hosting via Google Cloud Platform — `asia-southeast1` Singapore and `us-central1` regions), the **European Union** (analytics tooling under GDPR safeguards) and **Türkiye** (engineering operations of hangel Ltd. Şti., the group parent, under KVKK Law 6698). In line with section 26 PDPA and Part III of the PDP Regulations 2014, we take appropriate steps to ensure that the recipient is bound by **legally enforceable obligations** providing a standard of protection at least comparable to the PDPA — typically via the **ASEAN Model Contractual Clauses** (joined by IMDA in January 2021), the EU SCCs adapted for Singapore, or APEC CBPR certification where available. A copy of the safeguards is available on request to dpo-sg@hangel.org.

## 6. Your rights

Under sections 21 and 22 PDPA you may request: (i) access to your personal data and information about how it has been used or disclosed in the past 12 months; (ii) correction of inaccurate personal data; and (iii) — from 2024 onward — **data portability** to another organisation under the Tenth Schedule. We respond within **30 days** of a written request. A reasonable fee may be charged for access requests.

You may also **withdraw consent** at any time under section 16 PDPA by giving us reasonable notice; we will inform you of the likely consequences of withdrawal (e.g. inability to continue using certain features).

## 7. Retention and accuracy

Under section 25, we cease to retain personal data once the purpose for which it was collected is no longer being served and retention is no longer necessary for legal or business purposes. We retain account data for the life of your account plus 24 months for fraud prevention, server access logs for 12 months and tax records for **5 years** as required by section 67 of the Income Tax Act.

## 8. Data Breach Notification (Part VIA, sections 26A–26E)

Since 1 February 2021, we must notify the PDPC and affected individuals of a **notifiable data breach** — one that (a) is likely to result in *significant harm* to affected individuals, or (b) is of *significant scale* (affecting **500 or more individuals**). Notification to the PDPC is within **3 calendar days** of assessment that the breach is notifiable; affected individuals are notified concurrently unless an exception applies. We maintain an incident-response plan and a breach register.

## 9. Penalties

Under section 48J PDPA (in force from 1 October 2022), the PDPC may impose a financial penalty of up to **10% of the organisation's annual turnover in Singapore**, or **SGD 1 million**, whichever is higher, on organisations whose annual turnover in Singapore exceeds SGD 10 million. Smaller organisations face a maximum penalty of SGD 1 million. Under the Spam Control Act, sending unsolicited commercial electronic messages may attract SGD 25 per message in statutory damages.
